MAGIC ENEMY UPDATE

Included changes:
- Added magic_orb_spr and magic_target_spr (transparent, tintable sprite assets).
- Lucifer Cultist casts fire; Lucifer Necromancer casts dark/necrotic magic; Lucifer Skeleton King casts blue lightning.
- Legacy Skeleton Cultist casts earth; legacy Skeleton King casts light.
- Spell range is clamped to the existing sight_range and requires line of sight to begin a cast.
- Each cast snapshots the player's last visible position. The circle/star does not chase the player.
- Magic impact damages the player only if they are in the impact radius and routes through the existing player damage/parry handler.
- Ordinary Orcs, Warriors, and non-caster skeletons keep their regular melee attacks.

Magic is drawn from reusable sprites plus GameMaker draw primitives; element colors and impact shapes are configured in each magic enemy's Create_0.gml and Enemy_base_obj/Draw_0.gml.
